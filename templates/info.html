About TravelIndia

TravelIndia is your trusted companion for exploring the incredible diversity and beauty of India. We are dedicated to making travel planning simple, affordable, and memorable.

Our Mission

Our mission is to help travelers discover India's rich heritage, vibrant culture, and breathtaking landscapes while supporting local communities and tour guides.

What We Offer

  • Curated tour packages across India's most beautiful destinations
  • Expert local guides with deep knowledge of their regions
  • Affordable pricing with transparent booking
  • 24/7 customer support for all your travel needs
  • Authentic experiences that showcase the real India

Why Choose Us

We believe in responsible tourism that benefits both travelers and local communities. When you book with TravelIndia, you're not just getting a tour – you're supporting local guides, businesses, and sustainable travel practices.

Privacy Policy

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide when creating an account, booking tours, or contacting us. This includes your name, email, phone number, and travel preferences.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process your bookings and provide tour services
  • To communicate with you about your trips and updates
  • To improve our services and user experience
  • To send promotional offers (with your consent)

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information. Your data is encrypted and stored securely.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We only share data with tour guides and service providers necessary to fulfill your bookings.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal information at any time through your account settings or by contacting us.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a tour?

Simply browse our destinations, select a tour package, and fill out the booking form. You'll receive a confirmation email once your booking is processed.

Can I cancel or modify my booking?

Yes, you can cancel or modify your booking by contacting our support team. Cancellation policies vary by tour package.

Are the tour guides certified?

Yes, all our tour guides are experienced, knowledgeable locals who are passionate about sharing their culture and heritage.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept various payment methods including credit/debit cards, UPI, and net banking for your convenience.

Is travel insurance included?

Travel insurance is not automatically included but can be added to your booking. We recommend purchasing travel insurance for all trips.

What if I need help during my trip?

Our 24/7 support team is always available to assist you. You'll receive emergency contact numbers with your booking confirmation.
